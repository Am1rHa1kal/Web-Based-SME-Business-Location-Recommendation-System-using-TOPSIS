# Web-Based SME Business Location Recommendation System using TOPSIS

A web-based support system in decision by ranking business locations in Selangor district for SMEs business owner by using TOPSIS which include demographic presentation

## Problem statement
SME owners which reside or owners who plan to open business in Selangor often relies on personal judgement which only considered few criteria only. This business strategy often times lead to unsatisfied result since it ignored some important criteria that actually must be look before considering to choose it as business location. The lack of support system also contribute to issue for SMEs owner which make them stuck when progressing in finding suitable location.
